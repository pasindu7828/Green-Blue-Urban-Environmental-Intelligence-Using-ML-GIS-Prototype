# Forecast/XAI baseline update

- `Explain <year> prediction` now compares the selected forecast year with 2025, the latest observed year.
- `Explain this change` still compares consecutive forecast years, because it explains a year-to-year spike/drop.
- Explainable AI now contains a full Future Forecast Explanation section with zone, year and model selectors.
- Future explanation compares 2025 vs selected future year and shows feature changes plus prototype SHAP-style contribution changes.
- Historical trend-conditioned SHAP and historical Temporal SHAP remain as separate analysis sections.
- No Urbanization, Wetland or River research pages were edited.
